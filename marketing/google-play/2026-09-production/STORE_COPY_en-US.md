## Full description
One pitcher. More than one lifetime.

Challenge the batter with power—or paint the corner?
Train a little harder—or save your arm for the next outing?
In Mound Reborn, your choices build a baseball career. And when one career ends, the story can begin again.

Grow a pitcher through high school, chase a draft selection, and find your place in a fictional professional league. Take the mound yourself for the moments that matter, then choose a legacy to carry into your next baseball life.

PITCH THE BIG MOMENTS YOURSELF
Choose your pitch, location, and effort. Time your release with the pitching control and follow the ball to the glove. Trust the catcher's sign or make your own call—the anticipation is part of every pitch.

BUILD YOUR KIND OF PITCHER
Work on power, command, breaking pitches, or stamina. Recovery matters, too. Balance your training with your condition and the games ahead.

THREE YEARS. ONE DRAFT DAY.
Train, pitch, and make choices with coaches and teammates. Your high school record and growth come together when the draft arrives.

MAKE YOUR WAY IN THE PROS
Choose a contract, plan your weeks, and face decisions throughout the season. Shape your role and development, and step onto the mound for important games.

AN ENDING DOESN'T ERASE EVERYTHING
A disappointing career can still leave something worth keeping. Look back at your record, choose a signature legacy, and give your next pitcher a different starting point.

KEEP A CAREER WORTH REMEMBERING
Outing records, season stats, and your player album preserve the story you played. Every pitch becomes another part of it.

Mound Reborn takes place in an original fictional baseball world inspired by the regional culture of Korean baseball. It is not officially affiliated with real leagues, clubs, or players.

Offline-focused play, with no separate game account required. A paid full game with no ads or in-app purchases.
